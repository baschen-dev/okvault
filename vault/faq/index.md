# FAQ

* [How do I add an FAQ entry?](./how-do-i-add-an-faq.md) - Create a note of type FAQ whose title is the question and whose body is the answer.
* [How do I find the notes for a ticket?](./how-do-i-find-notes-for-a-ticket.md) - kb_ticket (or okvault ticket KEY) returns the ticket and every note linked to it, mentioning it, or matching its summary.
* [How do I resolve a merge conflict in index.md or log.md?](./how-do-i-resolve-a-merge-conflict.md) - Take either side of a generated index.md and run okvault index; log.md merges by union and okvault index tidies it.
* [How does kb_ask decide it has an answer?](./how-does-kb-ask-decide.md) - kb_ask answers only when the question matches an FAQ title or alias exactly, or overlaps it strongly and is covered by it.
